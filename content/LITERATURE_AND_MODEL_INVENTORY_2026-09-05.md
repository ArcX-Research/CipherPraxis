## Review boundary

The statuses distinguish a literature-backed construction from a control-qualified solver. Exact examples prove only the stated forward and inverse arithmetic. Finite-bank controls prove only their declared banks, lengths, and family cells. None of these pages turns a bounded target miss into a theorem about every variant.
